Our Story

A little steamer basket in Khanpur, now a neighbourhood favourite

Momo Magic Cafe started with one simple belief: momos taste best when they're folded by hand, steamed to order, and served the moment they leave the basket. What began as a small Khanpur counter has grown into a full cafe menu — without ever rushing the momos.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries that same promise. We measure our dough, we mix our chutneys fresh each morning, and we still count the pleats on every momo we fold.
